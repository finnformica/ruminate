### Changed

- The edit bar above a phone's keyboard moves as you use it. A tap gives under your finger and springs back; **Aa** turns into the **Back** button in place while the formatting buttons slide in one after another, and **Back** turns into **Aa** again the same way; **Turn into** brings the block types up from beneath; **Redo** grows in beside **Undo** once there is something to redo and shrinks away again; a button that goes grey fades rather than snapping. All of it stays still when your device asks for reduced motion.
