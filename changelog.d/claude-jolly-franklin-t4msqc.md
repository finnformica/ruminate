### Changed

- The edit bar above a phone's keyboard moves as you use it. A tap squeezes the button under your finger; **Aa** and **Turn into** slide their row in, and **Back** slides the main row back; **Redo** grows in beside **Undo** once there is something to redo and shrinks away again; a button that goes grey fades rather than snapping. All of it stays still when your device asks for reduced motion.
