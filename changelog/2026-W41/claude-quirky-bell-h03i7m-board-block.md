### Added

- A board inside a note. Type <kbd>/</kbd>board where you are writing to make a new board in place, or <kbd>/</kbd>link board to put one you already have there: the board sits in the note as a card — its name, how many pictures it holds and its features — with **Open board** in its corner. A board made this way is not listed under Views unless you add it. Removing the row leaves the board where it was; **Delete** in the row's menu deletes the board itself, after asking.
