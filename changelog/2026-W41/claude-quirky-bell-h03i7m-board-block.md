### Added

- A board inside a note. Type <kbd>/</kbd>board where you are writing and pick one of your boards from the list that opens — most recently updated first, narrowed as you type — or **Create new board** to make one there, named by what you typed. The board sits in the note as a card with its name and what it holds, and an arrow in its corner opens it. It is not listed under Views unless you add it. Removing the row leaves the board; **Delete** in the row's menu deletes it, after asking.
