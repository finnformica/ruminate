### Changed

- The fold chevron has a column of its own, and is always shown. A block with blocks under it shows its chevron at the left of the row, before its bullet, number, checkbox or `#`, open or closed, and the indent line beneath hangs from the chevron, so the control and the rows it folds read as one thread. Until now the chevron only appeared when you hovered a parent's marker, taking the marker's place, and a to-do's sat beside its checkbox.
- Every block with a line of text has a mark beside it. A paragraph now carries a faint `¶`, so a plain block is marked as a bullet, a number or a heading is. A picture, a link card and a code block keep an empty slot, since their frame is their mark.
- A block listed in the sidebar leads with the same mark as its row in the note. A bullet shows its dot rather than a dash, a to-do its box, a numbered item its number, a paragraph its `¶`, so the sidebar's Views list and the Views page read alike.

### Fixed

- A board listed among the notes shows the board icon. On the Views page, in the palette and wherever a note stands as a row, a board was drawn with the plain note icon; its row now shows the same icon the sidebar gives it.
