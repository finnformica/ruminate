### Added

- Rename or delete a board's values from the board. **Edit values** in a picker's menu, or the **Values** count in the Features editor, lists a feature's values: edit a name in place, or delete one — it comes off every picture that carried it, after a confirmation, and a picture left with nothing else goes back to Unassigned. Until now a value could only be renamed or removed in the note's outline.

### Changed

- No more trailing dots on menu items and progress words. "Saving", "Loading" and the Filter menu's **Choose a block** and **Type words** (formerly **Choose…** and **Type…**) read without them.

### Fixed

- A button's label no longer loses the tails of its letters. On a board, a picker showing a value such as "typography" cut the bottom off its g, p and y; the label now has the room the font needs.
