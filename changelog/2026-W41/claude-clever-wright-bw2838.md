### Changed

- The calendar shows what you wrote on each day. A day or a week is a page of its own now: open one and every note you changed on it is drawn with its changes marked — added rows in green with a `+`, removed rows in red with a `−`, a reworded row with the words that went and came. Switch between **By note** and **In order**. Before this update only each row's last save was kept, so earlier days show the rows last saved on them as added.
