### Changed

- The calendar shows what you wrote on each day. A day or a week is a page of its own: open one and every note you changed on it is drawn with its changes marked — added rows in green with a `+`, removed rows in red with a `−`, a reworded row with the words that went and came — and the unchanged stretches folded behind "n rows above" and "n rows below", which open a few at a time. Switch between **By note** and **In order**. Earlier days show the rows last saved on them as added.
