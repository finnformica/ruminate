### Changed

- The calendar shows what you wrote on each day. A day or a week is a page of its own now: open one and every note you changed on it is drawn with its changes marked — added rows in green with a `+`, removed rows in red with a `−`, a reworded row with the words that went and came, and the unchanged stretches folded. Switch between **By note** and **In order**. Days before this update are approximate: a row appears on the day it was last saved, marked `·`, without what changed in it.
