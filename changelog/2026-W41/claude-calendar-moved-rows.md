### Fixed

- On the calendar, a row moved under another parent now shows where it went. A picture given a different value on a board, or a row dragged under another heading, used to show under both the old parent and the new one with no mark on either; now it goes out in red where it stood and comes in in green where it landed, and a row that is still where it was and somewhere new as well is marked only in the new place.
- On the calendar, a row with a change beneath it can be folded again. Every such row still starts open so nothing the day did is hidden, but its chevron used to do nothing; now it folds like any other row, and the fold is remembered with the note's.
