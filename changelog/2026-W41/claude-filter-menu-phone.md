### Changed

- In the Filter and Sort menus, **Update to default** is now the strong button and **Reset to default** the quiet one. The two used to look alike. On a phone both menus are a little wider, and open against the screen's edge so their branches have the same room whichever button opened them.

### Fixed

- A phone's Filter and Sort menus no longer scroll sideways with a filter set, nor cut off their Update and Reset buttons. A row's value ("Todo, Done") used to push every row wider than the menu; now the row keeps its name whole and shortens the value instead, and the two saved-view buttons fit the menu's narrow column.
