### Added

- On a phone, a waiting update is announced above the page. A new version is now a line at the top of the screen with **Update** beside it, instead of a dot on the menu button and a row at the foot of the navigation drawer, below the Views list, that had to be scrolled to. Dismiss puts the line away until the app is next opened; the dot and the row stay.

### Changed

- The app looks for a new version each time it comes back to the foreground. It used to look once an hour, which on a phone mostly meant the next time it was opened from cold.
