### Changed

- Pasted notes now nest under their headings. When text copied from another app has a heading with nothing indented beneath it, everything up to the next heading of the same or a higher level is placed under it, so each section folds and moves as one. Copying within Ruminate keeps its layout exactly, and <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>V</kbd> still pastes plain text into one block.
