### Changed

- Log any past day straight from a tracker's Grid. A tap ticks or clears a tick series, and a value series opens the same field and − and + as the Today strip; the arrow keys move between days and series, and Enter or Space edits.
- The Grid names each month and marks each week and today. Every month starts under a divider with its name, such as "November 2026", which scrolls away with the divider; a year names this month and every second one. Each row reads "Mon 2", a thin line starts each week, and today's number is highlighted.
- The Heatmap names its months and weekdays and marks today. A straight line divides the months, with a week that crosses into a new month split in two; each month is named beside its line, with fewer names when they would crowd, the weekdays are named down the side, and a dot sits under today.
