### Changed

- Log any past day straight from a tracker's Grid. A tap ticks or clears a tick series, and a value series opens the same field and − and + as the Today strip; the arrow keys move between days and series, and Enter or Space edits.
- The Grid names each month and marks each week and today. Every month starts under a divider with its name, such as "November 2026", which stays in view as its days scroll; each row reads "Mon 2", a thin line starts each week, and today's number is highlighted.
