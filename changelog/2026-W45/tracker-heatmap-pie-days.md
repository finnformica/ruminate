### Changed

- A tracker Heatmap day with more than one series is a pie, an equal slice in each series's colour, in place of diagonal bands. The legend no longer has a **More than one** item.
- The Heatmap names the current month in bold, as it does today's weekday.
- The Heatmap's days to come have a faint grey fill under their dashed outline.
- The space above the Heatmap's month names and below its legend is now the same as at its sides.
