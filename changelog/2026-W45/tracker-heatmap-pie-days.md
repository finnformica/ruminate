### Changed

- A tracker Heatmap day shows a dot in the colour of each series logged on it, in place of a full colour fill or diagonal bands. Up to four series are medium dots in a 2×2 grid; five to nine are smaller dots in a 3×3 grid. A slip on a habit to avoid is its dot with a cross. The legend no longer has a **More than one** item.
- The Heatmap names the current month in bold, as it does today's weekday.
- The Heatmap's days to come have a faint grey fill under their dashed outline.
- The space above the Heatmap's month names and below its legend is now the same as at its sides.
