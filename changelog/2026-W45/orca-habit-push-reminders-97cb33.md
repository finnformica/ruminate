### Added

- Habit reminders. Turn on **Remind me to log my habits** under **Settings → Preferences → Reminders** and choose a time (20:00 if you do not). If a habit in your trackers has nothing logged today by that time, Ruminate sends a notification that names the missing habits. Tap it to open the tracker. Each device has its own setting. On an iPhone, add Ruminate to the Home Screen first (iOS 16.4 or later).
- **Send a test reminder now** sends a reminder to this device at once, so you can see that notifications work.
