### Changed

- The tracker Heatmap shows each series on a day as a small square instead of a dot. One series is one square, two to four are a 2×2 grid, and five to nine a 3×3 grid. All squares on a day are the same size.
- The Heatmap no longer marks a slip on a habit to avoid. The slip is a plain square in the habit's colour. The habit's own card keeps its cross.
- The Heatmap's squares are centred in their day, on a phone too.
