### Changed

- The tracker Heatmap shows each series on a day as a small square instead of a dot. One series is one square, two to four share a 2×2 grid, and five to nine a 3×3 grid. No part of the grid is left empty: when a cell has no series, the series next to it fills it. For example, with three series one takes the top half and two take a quarter each.
- The Heatmap no longer marks a slip on a habit to avoid. The slip is a plain square in the habit's colour. The habit's own card keeps its cross.
- The Heatmap's squares are centred in their day, on a phone too.
