### Changed

- The tracker Heatmap shows each series on a day as a small square instead of a dot. The squares fill the day: one series fills it, and with more series a short last row widens to fill the width. A day with one series looks the same as a day with several.
- The Heatmap no longer marks a slip on a habit to avoid. The slip is a plain square in the habit's colour. The habit's own card keeps its cross.
- On a phone, the Heatmap's squares are sharp and centred in their day.
