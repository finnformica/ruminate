### Changed

- A tracker Heatmap day with five series shows three small squares on top and two full-width rows below.
- On a phone, the squares of a Heatmap day with five to nine series have equal space between them.
- The small squares and bars of a tracker Heatmap day have rounder corners, closer to the corners of a day with one series.
