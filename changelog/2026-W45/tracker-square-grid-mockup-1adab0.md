### Changed

- A tracker Heatmap day with five series shows three small squares on top and two full-width rows below.
- On a phone, the squares of a Heatmap day with five to nine series have equal space between them.
- The small squares and bars of a tracker Heatmap day have rounder corners, closer to the corners of a day with one series.
- The space between the squares of a Heatmap day with five to nine series is smaller, and the squares are larger. Every day's squares stay symmetric.
- The tracker day dialog shows the date in its header, with "(Today)" or "(Yesterday)" after it when it applies. The second date line under the header is gone.
- On a desktop, each date in a tracker's Recent list stays at the top of the list while you scroll through that day's entries.
