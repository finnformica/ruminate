### Changed

- A tracker's Recent list groups each day's entries under one date. Every series logged that day sits together, with a line between days and none inside a day.
- A row of stats replaces the line of text and the streak badge at the top of a tracker. It shows the streak, the active days in the last 7 and the last 28, and all time.
- The tracker's Heatmap keeps its rows as far apart as its columns, on every device.

### Removed

- A tracker shows its Heatmap and its month as cards, and nothing else. The week, the year and the Grid are gone from the menu, so the Grid improvements earlier this week no longer apply; on a phone the menu picks Heatmap or Cards.
