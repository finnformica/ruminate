### Changed

- On a wide screen the tracker Heatmap is drawn for its size. Month names, weekday names and the legend are larger, and the lines between months are thicker.
- The Heatmap's days are the same squares as the series cards: the same rounded corners, today ring, colours and hover. The corners grow with the size of a day.
