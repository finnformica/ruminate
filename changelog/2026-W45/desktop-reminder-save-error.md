### Fixed

- When habit reminders can't be turned on, Settings › Notifications now says why, not just to check the connection. It names a push service the browser has turned off (in Brave, turn on **Use Google services for push messaging**), a page that needs an update or a reload, a closed permission prompt, or a save the server refused.
- **Send a test reminder now** goes to each of your devices with reminders on. It says how many it reached, and why any device failed.
- Reminders now turn on in Edge on Windows, whose push address can be longer than the server took.
