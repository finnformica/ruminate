### Added

- Open two notes side by side. On a wide screen, press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>\</kbd> or the split button in the note's header for a second pane, and press it again to close the pane you are in. Press <kbd>o</kbd> on a block to open it in the other pane, and press <kbd>g</kbd> then <kbd>p</kbd> to move between panes. Each pane has its own note, focus and selection, and an edit in one shows in the other at once. While two panes are open, a note from the sidebar or the command menu opens in the pane you are in. A phone shows one pane.

### Changed

- Stepping back out of a focus selects the block you had selected before you went in. <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> and the breadcrumb now return you to each earlier view with its block selected, and its filter and folds as you left them. A new focus starts without a filter and with the usual folds. Reload, and the browser's back and forward buttons, keep the whole trail.
