### Added

- You can add pictures while you are offline. The picture shows in the note at once, with a **Waiting to upload** badge, and is kept on your device. It uploads by itself when you are back online, also if you close the app first.

### Fixed

- Focusing on a folded heading shows what is under it. Before, a top-level heading that was folded in the note showed nothing when you focused on it, until you unfolded it first.
