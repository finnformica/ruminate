### Added

- Sort your notes, and drag them into any order you like. The control beside **Notes** in the sidebar sorts by name, by most recently updated, or **Manual** — which lets you drag the rows into whatever order you want. **Move up** and **Move down** in a note's menu do the same without a mouse. Your choice applies to the sidebar and the notes page together.
- <kbd>g</kbd> then <kbd>c</kbd> opens the changelog.

### Changed

- Notes are sorted by name rather than by when you last touched them. Sorting by recent activity meant the note you were typing in climbed to the top while you were looking at it, so both lists reshuffled as you worked. Choose **Recently updated** in the new sort control to have the old behaviour back.
- The notes page splits your notes into the same sections as the sidebar. **Pinned**, **Notes** and **Shared** were one undifferentiated list, with nothing to tell your own notes from the ones shared with you. Searching still returns a single list, ranked by how well each result matches.
- **Pinned** heads both the sidebar and the notes page, and holds pinned notes and pinned blocks together. Pinned notes used to sit at the top of the notes with a marker, while pinned blocks had a list of their own further down — so what you had kept to hand was in two places, neither of them first. Every row there now leads with the pin, whether it is a note or a block, so the list reads as one.
- A pinned note shows the pin in place of its own icon throughout the sidebar. It used to keep its icon and carry a small pin beside its name, so a row said the same thing twice; one glance down the sidebar now says which notes are pinned.
- A pinned note keeps its place in the notes list as well. Pinning no longer lifts a note to the top, so the order is entirely the one you sorted or dragged into, and any note can be dragged anywhere.
- **What's new** is called **Changelog**, and sits with **Help** at the foot of the sidebar. The two of them explain the app, so they are together now, below the places you can go.

### Fixed

- The sidebar no longer highlights a note while you are focused on a block inside it. Focusing on a pinned block lit both the block's row and its note's, which read as being in two places at once; the row you are actually on is now the only one lit. A pinned note open at its top is still lit in both **Pinned** and **Notes** — those two rows are the same note, and each saying so is worth knowing.
- A pinned row's pin keeps its colour when you open it. It used to take on the colour of the selected row, so the one mark that says a row is pinned stopped looking like a pin exactly where you were reading.
