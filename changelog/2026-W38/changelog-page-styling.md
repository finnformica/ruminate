### Changed

- The changelog shows one week at a time. The weeks down the side pick one rather than marking where you had scrolled to, so **What's new** opens on the newest and stays there — no endless page running on beneath it, and no waiting while the rest is drawn. A link to a week still opens on that week.
- A change on the changelog now reads as a heading with its detail beneath. The sentence saying what changed used to open the paragraph in bold, which made a busy week one wall of text; it is now a line of its own, a size up, over the detail. Each week's dates head the page properly, and **Added**, **Fixed** and the rest are quiet labels between the changes rather than competing with them.
- On a narrow screen the changelog's weeks are a menu rather than a row of tabs scrolled sideways. The row put most of the weeks off the edge of the screen and asked for a sideways swipe to find out what was even there; one button naming the week you are reading opens the lot.
- The changelog gives the reading column the width as soon as the page narrows. It used to hold space for the list of weeks until the whole window was small, so with the sidebar open on a laptop the changes were squeezed into a strip; the weeks now lie across the top instead and the changes have the page to themselves.

### Fixed

- A change on the changelog is no longer shown two or three times over. Moving between weeks left the entries of the week you had just left behind, so the list grew each time and a change could turn up under a week it was not part of. Reopening the page cleared it, which is why it came and went.
- The newest week no longer disappears from the changelog's list the moment you scroll. The list shifted as the page moved and the top of it slid under the fade at the top of the page; it now sits where it stays.
