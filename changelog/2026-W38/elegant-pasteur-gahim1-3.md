### Added

- A block held in more than one place shows its other parents beneath it, so a note shows the graph, not only the tree. Zoom into a block copied under several to-dos and those to-dos are rows beneath it; open one and its note is beneath it in turn. A parent row edits like any other: retype or tick it and the change shows everywhere. Enter after it makes a new block holding the one above, indenting it moves what it holds, and deleting the row unlinks that parent.
- A setting chooses which links a note follows. **Settings → Editor → Show links** offers **Both** (the default), **Downstream** (what a block holds, as before) and **Upstream** (what holds it).
