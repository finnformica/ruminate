### Changed

- The edit bar above a phone's keyboard moves as you use it. A tap gives under your finger and springs back; **Aa** turns into the **Back** button in place while the formatting buttons slide in one after another, and **Back** turns into **Aa** again the same way; **Turn into** brings the block types up from beneath; **Redo** grows in beside **Undo** once there is something to redo and shrinks away again; a button that goes grey fades rather than snapping. All of it stays still when your device asks for reduced motion.
- The block menu on a touch screen is a sheet that rises from the bottom. Press and hold a block and the sheet opens with the block's text at the top and every action beneath, where the popup used to open and shut again as the finger lifted. It offers exactly what the right-click menu offers; a pick closes it, as does a swipe down, and the hold selects no text along the way.
- A phone's keyboard can delete a block again. Backspace at the start of a block strips its marker, then merges it into the block above, on keyboards that never say which key was pressed, however they stay quiet about it.
- **Delete**, **Undo** and **Redo** on the edit bar keep you editing. The keyboard and the bar stay up, where they used to drop you to a highlighted block; on a desktop, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> while editing keeps you editing too.
- A touch screen no longer lights a block once you stop editing it. The highlight only appears while the block's menu is open, and quietly.
- **Indent**, **Outdent**, **Move up** and **Move down** are in a block's right-click menu on every device, with their keys beside them. They used to appear only on a touch screen.

### Fixed

- Reopening the app on a phone with no network now opens your notes and keeps what you write. The database engine was left out of what the app keeps for offline use, so a cold start offline could not open the notes at all, and a new note went nowhere.
