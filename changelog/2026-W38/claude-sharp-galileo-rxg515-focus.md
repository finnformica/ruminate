### Changed

- "Zoom" is called **Focus** everywhere. The keys are unchanged — <kbd>f</kbd> focuses a block, <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>f</kbd> steps back one level, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>.</kbd> leaves — but the block menu now says **Focus on**, the shortcuts panel groups them under **Focus**, and a block's bullet focuses rather than zooms. Focusing on a block makes it the root of what you see, which is what the word should have said all along. Links you have already saved still work.
