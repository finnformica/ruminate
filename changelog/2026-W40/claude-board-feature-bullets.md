### Changed

- Boards: a feature made from the form — **Location**, **Object**, **Material**, **Link** — is now a bullet in the note, not a paragraph. A feature block already on the page is left as it is.

### Fixed

- A paragraph with blocks beneath it now shows its collapse chevron while it is open. A paragraph has no bullet or other mark for the chevron to take the place of, so an open paragraph parent used to show nothing in its margin at all until the pointer found the spot.
