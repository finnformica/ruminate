### Changed

- Pictures load as you scroll to them, in notes and on boards alike, and show a small copy first. Until a picture arrives its place shows a blurred likeness of it; a small copy fades in over that, and where the picture is shown large — a wide figure in a note, the expanded view, a board's inspector — the full picture takes the copy's place once it is ready. The copy is made as a picture is added; a picture added before this gets one the first time it is shown.
