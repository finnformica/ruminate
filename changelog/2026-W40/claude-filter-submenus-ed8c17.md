### Fixed

- A note's Filter and Sort menus open their branches beside them, where a phone used to drop a branch straight over the menu it came from. A branch opens flush against the menu, its first row level with the row that opened it, on whichever side has the room; on a phone the menu keeps to a narrow column so the two fit side by side. Rest on a row to open its branch with a mouse; on a phone, tap it, tap it again to close it, or tap another row to open that one instead.
