### Changed

- A board's Filter narrows by every feature at once. Tick a value under Location and another under Fixture and the wall keeps the pictures that carry both, where it used to keep the ones that carried either; ticks under one feature still widen it (Mauritius or Lisbon). The same holds typed by hand anywhere a search is run: `parent:a parent:b` and `under:a under:b` are the blocks under both, and `parent:a,b` is still either.
