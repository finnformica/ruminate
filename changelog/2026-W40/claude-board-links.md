### Added

- Boards: a picture can carry a link to where it came from. **Link** in a picture's window takes the address of the product or page, and shows it as a card with the page's title and description and a control to open it. Pictures from one page share the card, the Filter menu lists the board's links beside its other features, and in the note the picture sits under its card beneath a **Link** block, as it does under any other value.
