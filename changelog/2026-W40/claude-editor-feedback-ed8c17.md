### Changed

- In a filtered note, the row you are editing stays on the page whatever the filter says of it. It is judged once you leave it: make a bullet a to-do, strip a to-do's marker with <kbd>⌫</kbd>, outdent a row or type a line that does not match, and it stays under the caret until the editing moves off it, then goes if it no longer matches. A row that stopped matching used to vanish on the keystroke that changed it, so nothing could be made that the filter would not have shown.
- A note's header stays on **Saving…** for as long as you are typing, and settles once you pause. It used to flip between "Saving…" and what came after it on every keystroke, which flickered on a phone. On a phone, an edit waiting for the network now reads **Saved** with the offline icon, where it used to spell out "Saved offline".
- A board's ⋯ menu says **Open note** where it used to say "Open outline".
