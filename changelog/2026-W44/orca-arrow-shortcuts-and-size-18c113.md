### Changed

- Typing an arrow now writes the arrow itself. <kbd>-</kbd><kbd>></kbd> gives →, <kbd><</kbd><kbd>-</kbd> gives ←, <kbd><</kbd><kbd>-</kbd><kbd>></kbd> gives ↔, <kbd>=</kbd><kbd>></kbd> gives ⇒, <kbd><</kbd><kbd>=</kbd> gives ⇐ and <kbd><</kbd><kbd>=</kbd><kbd>></kbd> gives ⇔, so copy, search and export keep it. <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> straight after gives back what you typed. Code stays as typed.
- <kbd>⌫</kbd> straight after an arrow appears gives back what you typed, as in Word. A later <kbd>⌫</kbd>, or one after you move the cursor, deletes the arrow.
- Arrows are larger and clearer. They used to be narrow and small beside the text; now they are full size, near the arrows in Notion.

### Fixed

- A two-way arrow typed as `<->` shows as ↔. Before, it showed as `<` and then →.
