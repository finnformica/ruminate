### Changed

- Syncing is lighter. Each sync now carries only what changed since the last one, so a sync with nothing new costs almost nothing, and a save costs a fixed small amount however many notes you have.
- A new device shows your notes sooner. On first sign-in it asks for the list of your notes first and draws it at once, then takes one copy of your notes as they are now and fills in the history, both behind the screen.
- On a new device the notes page keeps its loading skeleton until your own notes are there. It no longer shows only the notes shared with you for a few seconds first.
