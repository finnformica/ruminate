### Added

- A tracker can now show a week or a whole year as well as a month. The arrows move one week, month or year at a time.
- A tracker can now show its days as one grid: a row for each series and a column for each day, like a habit page in a paper journal. Tap a day in the grid to log it, as on the cards. On a phone the days scroll sideways and the series names stay in view.

### Fixed

- Unticking a day in a tracker now removes that day's row from the note. Before, the row stayed in the note as an open to-do.
