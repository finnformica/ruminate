### Added

- Trackers: a note that keeps a habit or a number day by day. **New tracker** in the header's **New** menu makes one and opens it as a month grid, one card per series, each day a cell filled in the series's colour by how much was logged. The **Today** strip logs the day. **Series** in the ⋯ menu adds a series and sets its kind, colour, unit, step and target. In the note itself the log reads as text: a ticked to-do with the date for a habit, a line such as 2026-10-28 · 5 km for a number.
