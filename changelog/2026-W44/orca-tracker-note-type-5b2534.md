### Added

- Trackers: a note that keeps a habit or a number day by day. **New tracker** in the header's **New** menu makes one and opens it as a month grid, one card per series, each day a cell filled in the series's colour by how much was logged. The **Today** strip at the top logs the day in a tap. **Series** in the ⋯ menu adds a series and sets its kind, colour, unit, step and target. Each card shows the streak, the month's total and how far towards the target. The grid fits a phone.
