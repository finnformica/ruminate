### Added

- **Pin** a note or a block from its menu to keep it to hand. Pinned notes and blocks show under **Pinned** in the sidebar and in the ⌘K palette. **Unpin** takes one off.

### Changed

- **Notes** is now the one list of your notes, in the order you drag them into. The sidebar shows the same list under **Notes**, below **Pinned**. Search and the calendar follow that order too.
- A note's address starts with `/notes/`. Old links that start with `/views/` still open the same note or block.

### Removed

- The **Views** list in the sidebar and the palette, with its sort menu. Pin what you want to keep to hand.
- **Add to Views**, **Remove from Views** and **Save as view**.
- A note no longer saves a filter or a sort. A filter or a sort you set is in the address, so a link or a bookmark keeps it.
