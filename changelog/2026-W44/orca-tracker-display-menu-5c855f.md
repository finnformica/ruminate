### Changed

- A tracker's week, month or year and its cards or grid are now chosen from a display button at the top right, beside the ⋯ menu. Before, two rows of buttons sat above the days, and on a phone they ran off the screen.
- A tracker's grid is now turned on its side: a row for each day and a column for each series. It opens at today's row. A column shows only its series's colour, so the series at the top of the page act as the key, and a phone shows six series without scrolling sideways.
