### Added

- A tracker now opens with a summary line. It shows your streak of days with any entry, then how many of the last 7 days, the last 28 days and all days have one.
- A tracker now shows a heatmap of the last 12 weeks. Each column is a week, and today is at the right. A day takes the colour of each series logged on it, split when there is more than one. Below it, the Recent list shows the newest entries with their text, five at first and more with Show more.
- On a phone the heatmap and Recent list are what a tracker opens to. Pick a week, month or year from the display button to see the cards or the grid, and tap Heatmap to come back. On a wide screen both are shown, side by side.
