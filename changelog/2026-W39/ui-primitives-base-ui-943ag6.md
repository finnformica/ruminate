### Changed

- Dialogs and the full-size image view open and close with the same motion as the app's menus. They used to appear and vanish in place; the share dialog now settles in and fades back out, and the picture's scrim fades with it.
- A dialog sits in the middle of the page and dims what is behind it. It used to hang near the top over an undimmed page, so it was easy to lose among the note; the palette dims the page the same way.
