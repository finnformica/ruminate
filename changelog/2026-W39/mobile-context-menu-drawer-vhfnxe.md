### Changed

- The block menu is shorter. It no longer repeats what the block already offers elsewhere: Edit, Indent, Outdent, Collapse, Focus on and Turn into are gone, since a tap or click edits, the arrow collapses, and the rest are on the editing bar on a phone and on the keyboard on a desktop. A block with a link in it offers Turn into link block on its own.

### Fixed

- Holding a block on a phone to open its menu no longer selects text, or presses the menu item under your finger as you lift it. Nothing on the page or in the menu is selected while you hold. Holding the block you are editing selects text as usual instead of opening the menu.
