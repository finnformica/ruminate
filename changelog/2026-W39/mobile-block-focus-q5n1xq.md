### Added

- Focus on any block from your phone's edit bar. Tap a row to edit it, then **Focus on** in the bar above the keyboard makes that block and everything under it the whole view — a paragraph, a heading or a parent as readily as a list item, which until now was the only block a finger could focus on, by its bullet. The keyboard goes so the view is there to read; the breadcrumb or Back takes you out again.
