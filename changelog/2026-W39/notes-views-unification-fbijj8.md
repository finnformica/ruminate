### Changed

- Notes and Views are one list. The sidebar, the home page and the ⌘K palette list every note and every block you have made a view of under one heading, **Views**, in the order the sort menu says. There is no separate Notes list any more, and nothing is pinned: to keep something to hand, switch the sort to **Manual** and drag it to the top. A block can sit between two notes.
- A note opens at `/views/<id>`. Links to the old `/notes/<id>` address still work, and open the same note with any focused block, filter or sort they carried.
- **Add to Views** replaces **Pin** on a block. It is in the block's right-click menu, and on a phone in the block's sheet; **Remove from Views** takes the block out again, its saved filter and sort with it. The pin glyph is gone from block rows and from the sidebar, and a block view's row in the sidebar leads with the block's own markdown marker instead — a bullet's `-`, a to-do's `[ ]`, a heading's `#`.
- The Views list is reached with **g** then **v**. It used to be **g** then **n**.

### Removed

- Pinning a note. Every note is in the Views list already, so there was nothing left for the pin to do; a note's **⋯** menu and its preview card no longer offer it.
