### Fixed

- Right-clicking a row on the Views page opens a menu again. A browsed row — on the Views page and in the ⌘K palette, and on a phone with a press-and-hold — offers **Open**, **Copy**, **Copy link to block** and **Add to Views** (or **Remove from Views**), and none of the editing the note's own menu carries. Before, a right-click there showed the browser's menu instead.
- A sidebar row's **⋯** menu no longer flashes in the top-left corner as it closes. Clicking away from an open menu briefly drew it at the page's corner; it now fades out where it was.
