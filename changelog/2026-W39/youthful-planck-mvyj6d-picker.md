### Added

- Filter a note by what a row sits under, from its header. **Parent** and **Under** in the Filter menu open the search palette over the note's own rows: pick a row and the note narrows to what is beneath it, or take the typed text as "contains" and it narrows to what is beneath every row saying that. Stack either with **Type** for, say, one person's open to-dos in a standup.
