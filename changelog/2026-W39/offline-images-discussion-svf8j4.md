### Added

- Pictures you have opened are kept on your device, so they still show when you are offline. Pictures are saved as you view or add them, up to 250 MB, with the earliest-saved ones making way for new ones.

### Changed

- A picture that has not loaded yet shows a blurred preview of itself, at its final size, rather than a grey box. This applies to pictures added from now on; older ones keep the grey box.
- A picture that cannot be fetched keeps its place in the note, with a small **Offline** badge, and loads by itself when the connection returns. Before, it was replaced by "Image unavailable", which is now kept for pictures that no longer exist.
