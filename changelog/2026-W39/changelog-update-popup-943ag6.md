### Changed

- Raised surfaces settle for a plain fade when your system asks for less motion. Menus, tooltips, hover cards and the what's-new card no longer grow and shrink as they open and close if you have reduced motion turned on — the fade stays, since it is what tells you something has arrived or gone.

### Fixed

- The what's-new card now arrives and leaves like the rest of the app. In most browsers it appeared with no animation at all, and dismissing it took it away mid-blink; it fades and scales into its corner, and back out of it, the way every menu and tooltip does.
