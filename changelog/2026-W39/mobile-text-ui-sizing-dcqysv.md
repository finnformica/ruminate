### Changed

- Notes are easier to read and tap on a phone. Text is a size larger, the todo checkbox and the collapse arrow are bigger, and there is more room between a checkbox and its text.

### Fixed

- Tapping just before a to-do's text on a phone puts the cursor at the start of the line instead of ticking the box. A tap in the space beside any block's text used to put the cursor at the end of the text; it now goes to the start of that line.
