### Removed

- Clicking a bullet or a list number no longer focuses on its block. On a phone the dot sat where a finger reaching for the text landed, so a tap kept swapping the note for one block; on a desktop it was a target nobody meant to hit. Focus on a block with <kbd>F</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>.</kbd>, **Focus on** in its right-click menu, or **Focus on** in the phone's edit bar.

### Fixed

- The breadcrumb keeps every step of a focus. Focusing on a block and then on one inside it showed only the note and the current block; the trail now names each block you came through, and each crumb takes you back to that step.
