### Changed

- The help panel slides in and out on a wide screen. It used to appear and vanish in place; now it opens and closes with its contents sliding in from the page's edge and back out, and its edge travels with them rather than jumping ahead.
- The share dialog says what the shared note can have done to it. Its permissions are summed up as "Shared note and what is beneath it can be read and edited", rather than as what the other person can do, and the hint under the email field and the heading over the permissions are gone.
