### Added

- The notes page now lists your **Recent** places above your Views. It is the same list the ⌘K palette shows before you type, so the notes and blocks you keep coming back to are one click away from either.

### Changed

- Recent now remembers the block you focused on, not just the note it lives in. Unfold Personal, focus on Fashion, and Fashion itself becomes a Recent row that opens straight onto it.
- Recent now ranks by how often you come back to a place as well as how lately. A place you return to day after day stays near the top, and one visit somewhere else no longer pushes it out. Repeated edits within one sitting count as a single visit.
- A pinned note or block that you use often now appears under both Recent and Views in the ⌘K palette. Before, it was listed under Recent only.
- The notes page no longer lists every note beneath Recent and Views. Recent and Views now hold the places you come back to, and the sidebar and search reach the rest. A new account with nothing recent or pinned still sees its notes listed.
